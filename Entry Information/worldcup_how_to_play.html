How to Win

Pick your winners. Call your losers. Score points every match day.

How to Play

1

Most points wins the pool

Points accumulate all tournament long — across every group stage match, every knockout game, and the final. Whoever has the highest total score when the final whistle blows wins.

2

Pick 5 winners and 5 losers — one from each pot

All 48 teams are divided into five pots by odds. You pick exactly one winner and one loser from each pot. That gives you 10 teams total. Your winner and loser in each pot must be different teams.

3

Your winners score when they win — your losers score when they lose

Every time a team on your winner list wins a match, you earn that pot's win points. Every time a team on your loser list loses a match, you earn that pot's loss points. Wrong results score zero — no penalties.

4

Points multiplier kicks in after the group stage

Group stage matches score at 1× face value. Once the knockout rounds begin, every match scores at 3×. The Final scores at 5×. Teams that go deep are worth a lot more — the tournament never stops mattering.

Scoring

✅ Winner picks

You earn the pot's win points every time your winner wins a match. Teams that go deep keep scoring. A Pot 3 or 4 team on a group stage roll can rack up serious points across three games.

❌ Loser picks

You earn the pot's loss points every time your loser loses a match. Elite teams lose matches regularly — even the favorites drop one in the group stage. A Pot 1 loss pick (50 pts) rewards you for spotting a stumble.

Draws score nothing. A tied group stage match does not count as a win or loss for scoring purposes. Only decisive results earn points.

The Rules

🔴You cannot pick the same team as both your winner and loser. You must commit to one view per team.
🟡You must pick exactly one winner and one loser from each pot — no skipping pots, no doubling up within a pot.
🟢Picks are locked before the tournament starts. You cannot change your roster once the group stage begins on June 11.
🔵Group stage matches score at 1×. All knockout rounds score at 3×. The Final scores at 5×.
⚪Draws score nothing for either winner or loser picks. Only decisive match results count.
🟠In knockout rounds, a win by extra time or penalty shootout counts as a win for the winning team and a loss for the eliminated team. Points are scored normally.
⚫There are no penalties for wrong results. If your winner loses or your loser wins, you simply score zero for that match.

Tiebreaker

If two or more players finish with the same total score, the tiebreaker is a closest-guess contest:

⚽ How many goals will Lionel Messi score in the 2026 World Cup?

Each player submits a whole number guess on their pick sheet. In the event of a tied final score, the player whose Messi guess is closest to his actual tournament goal tally wins. If two players are equally close, the higher guess wins.

Strategy Tips

The group stage is where you build your lead. With 72 matches across 12 groups, there's something on the line almost every day for three weeks. Teams that win two or three group games will generate the most points for winner picks.

Your Pot 1 loser is almost guaranteed to score. Every Elite team loses at least once — even Spain, France, and Brazil stumble in the group stage regularly. Fifty points per loss adds up fast across three group games.

Don't ignore Pot 2. Dark Horse teams like USA, Morocco, and Colombia often win 2-3 group games at 50 pts each. That's 150 points from the group stage alone — more than any Elite winner pick can generate.

Pot 5 is a lottery ticket. A 350-point win is extraordinary but rare. Pick a Wildcard winner for the thrill, not the expectation.

Think about group matchups. Check the Groups & Points sheet — a Pot 3 team in a weak group has a much better chance of winning two or three games than one drawn against Elite opposition.
