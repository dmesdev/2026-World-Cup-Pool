What it means

Australia–Egypt opens the day with the biggest single-match payout potential of the entire Round of 32. Five players — Tim, Corge, Matt, Monica, and Rob & Monica White — are all chasing the 1,050-point Australia upset, staking a combined 5,250 points on the Socceroos. Dave Mestres is the only player taking the other side, backing Egypt to win for 600. Corge Umlauf doubles up with both Australia to win and Egypt to lose — compatible picks since an Australia win means Egypt loses, so both cash together for 1,080 points.

Argentina–Cabo Verde is the quietest match of the day with just two picks and a clean head-to-head: Monica Mestres needs Argentina to win for 30 points, while Tim Mestres needs Argentina to lose for 150. The 1,050-point Cabo Verde upset goes unclaimed — the second consecutive match with a four-figure wildcard payout that nobody in the pool is chasing.

Colombia–Ghana closes out the Round of 32 with an interesting split. Four players — Deb, Corge, Camden, and Payton — are backing Colombia to win for 150 each, while five players are fading Ghana to lose for 30. Corge Umlauf carries the day's only conflict: he holds both Colombia to win (150) and Ghana to win (600) in the same match, meaning only one can score regardless of result. A Ghana upset would net Corge 600 but wipe out his Colombia bet; a Colombia win delivers the reverse. Deb and Payton have clean reinforcing doubles — Colombia to win and Ghana to lose are the same outcome for both.

The biggest potential day belongs to Tim Mestres: Australia to win (1,050) and Argentina to lose (150) in separate matches gives him a possible 1,200 points if both results land. Monica Mestres is close behind — Australia win (1,050) + Argentina win (30) + Ghana lose (30) = 1,110 points — though her Argentina and Tim's Argentina picks are on opposite sides of that game.
