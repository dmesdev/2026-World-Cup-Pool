## How It Works

Each player picks **5 teams to win** and **5 teams to lose** across tiered groups based on odds. Points are awarded for correct picks, scaled by round multipliers.

### Round Multipliers

| Stage | Multiplier |
|---|---|
| Group Stage | 1× |
| All Knockout Rounds (R32–SF) | 3× |
| Final | 5× |

### Tiebreaker

Closest guess to Lionel Messi's actual goal total for the tournament.

> Extra time and penalty shootout results count as a win/loss in knockout rounds.
